About Us

Sinsina Industrial Works (SIW) operates out of our Jubail Industrial City facilities. Our manufacturing scope brings together engineered skid packages, static equipment, instrumentation and flow control, and advanced metallurgy.

01

Modular Process & Integrated Skids

Including Gas Modular Packages (Saudi Aramco LTA Program), Chemical Injection Skids, and Surge Relief Skids.

02

Static Equipment Fabrication

Atmospheric storage tanks, pressure vessels, and heavy structural frames.

03

Instrumentation & Flow Control

Complete E&I integration, testing, and commissioning.

04

Advanced Metallurgy Expertise

Specialized welding and fabrication in Inconel 625 alloys, 316 & 430 grade Stainless Steel, and carbon steel.
