About Us

Saudi engineering, fabrication and industrial integration capabilities built around precision, execution discipline and field-ready delivery.

SINSINA INDUSTRIAL WORKS JUBAIL · SAUDI ARABIA

Industrial capability, integrated under one roof.

Sinsina Industrial Works (SIW) is a leading Saudi industrial manufacturing partner headquartered in Jubail Industrial City. We specialize in end-to-end engineering, precision shop fabrication, modular skid assembly, and seamless equipment integration. Guided by strict international quality codes, client specifications, and practical field requirements, our work ensures optimal operational performance and reliability from shop testing through site commissioning.

Core Foundations of Our Operations

Turnkey Engineering & Technical Mastery

At SIW, industrial execution begins with robust, multidisciplinary engineering. Our team bridges conceptual design, detailed mechanical calculations, 3D modelling, and piping layout to deliver fully integrated process packages. By managing interfaces in-house, we eliminate design mismatches, streamline fabrication, and guarantee field compatibility.

Advanced Shop Fabrication

Operating from our heavy industrial manufacturing facilities in Jubail, we maintain specialized fabrication yards equipped for high-precision welding, CNC machining, rolling machine and heavy structural assembly. Our deep metallurgic expertise encompasses high-performance alloys and corrosion-resistant materials—including Inconel 625, 316 & 430 Stainless Steel, and high-yield carbon steel.

Comprehensive Modular & Package Integration

We deliver shop-tested, plug-and-play modular solutions that minimize field construction risks, footprint requirements, and site downtime. From complete structural skid bases to process piping, valves, electrical wiring, and instrumentation (E&I), every package is integrated and systematically tested under one roof.

Uncompromised Quality & Field Reliability

Quality is embedded across every phase of our manufacturing lifecycle. Operating under international quality control codes and ISO frameworks, SIW conducts comprehensive Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT), pressure testing, and non-destructive testing (NDT) to ensure field-ready, compliant equipment.

Sinsina Industrial Works commenced operations in 2019 in Jubail, Saudi Arabia, with a focus on engineered skid packages and industrial fabrication. Since its establishment, SIW has continued to build its engineering and manufacturing capabilities to serve the evolving requirements of the energy and industrial sectors.

ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management Systems

Our end-to-end manufacturing workflows—from raw material receiving and metallurgical verification to final assembly— are strictly governed by certified ISO quality management protocols.

Advanced NDT & Inspection

Every weldment, structural frame, and piping assembly undergoes Radiographic (RT), Ultrasonic (UT), Magnetic Particle (MT), and Liquid Penetrant (PT) testing.

FAT & Hydrotesting

Fully integrated packages undergo pressure testing, functional instrumentation checks, and client FAT in-shop prior to dispatch.

ISO 45001 & ISO 14001

We maintain international compliance across Occupational Health & Safety and Environmental Management Systems, supporting a proactive “Zero Harm” workplace environment.

Strict Shop & Site Protocols

Our facilities enforce rigorous risk assessment, PPE requirements, hot-work safety controls, and continuous safety training.

Sustainable Manufacturing

We minimize industrial footprint through controlled waste management, efficient energy utilization, and safe material disposal during fabrication and surface treatment operations.

Proudly Part of Sinsina Holding.

As a part of Sinsina Holding, SIW is proud to hold Saudi Aramco’s Long-Term Agreement (LTA) program for GMS packages, delivering engineered skid packages and fabricated structures with a focus on quality and reliability.
